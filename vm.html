Get the most out of it
TIP 01

Use fullscreen for real work

Hit the Fullscreen button above (or F11 after) — it hides our page entirely and gives the VM the whole screen. From a distance, it just looks like a Windows desktop.

TIP 02

The clipboard is separate

Copy/paste between the VM and your real computer often doesn't work. If you need to move a link or text in, type it manually or use a paste site inside the VM's browser.

TIP 03

Free machines = queues

Free VMs are shared and sometimes there's a wait or a usage timer. If one OS is busy, try another Windows option in the list — they behave the same.

TIP 04

Don't log into personal accounts on the host

The whole point is separation. Do your personal stuff inside the VM only. Logging in on the school laptop itself defeats the entire purpose.

TIP 05

Files don't survive

When the session ends, the VM resets to factory state. Need something later? Upload it to cloud storage inside the VM before you close the tab.

TIP 06

Close it properly

Shut down the VM from its start menu or just close the tab — either works. But check nothing important is open first, because there's no getting it back.

Something not working?
The embed is blank or won't load
Some school networks or extensions block embedded content. Hit Reload first; if it stays blank, use the New tab button — the same VM runs there, just outside our page.
The VM is slow or laggy
That's normal — you're streaming a remote computer. Close other tabs, avoid fullscreen video inside the VM, and give it a minute after booting before doing anything heavy.
It says "all machines are busy"
Free servers fill up at peak hours. Wait a few minutes and refresh, or try again during off-peak times. You can also try a different OS from the same list.
My keyboard/mouse won't work in the VM
Click once inside the VM screen first — the browser needs to give it focus. If it still ignores you, reload the frame and click into the desktop as soon as it appears.
