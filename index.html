About

This site was built because school tools shouldn't be hard to find. We're a small group of students who got tired of digging through sketchy forums and dead links just to find something that actually works.

Everything here is community-driven — students submit what works, we verify it, and it goes up. No ads, no paywalls, no bullshit.

If you want to contribute, you can open a pull request on our GitHub repository and leave a star if you find it helpful.
