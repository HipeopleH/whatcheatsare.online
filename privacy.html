🔒 Privacy Policy

How we handle your data, what we collect, and your rights.

1. Information We Collect

We do not collect any personal information by default. This site is a static website hosted on GitHub Pages, and we do not run any analytics, tracking scripts, or cookies.

However, your browser may automatically send certain information (such as your IP address, browser type, and referring page) to GitHub's servers as part of standard HTTP requests. This is beyond our control and is governed by GitHub's Privacy Policy.

2. Third-Party Services

This site links to external services including, but not limited to:

  • Discord — for community discussions and support
  • GitHub — for hosting and source code management
  • Various tool websites — linked in the tool cards (each has its own privacy policy)

We are not responsible for the privacy practices of these third-party services. Please review their privacy policies before using them.

3. Cookies

This site does not use cookies or any other tracking mechanisms. We believe in a no-bullshit approach to privacy — no tracking, no ads, no selling your data.

4. Data Security

Since we don't collect any data, there's nothing to secure. However, all connections to this site are encrypted via HTTPS (provided by GitHub Pages), ensuring that your browsing activity is protected from eavesdropping.

5. User Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access, modify, or delete any data we might have about you (which is none)
  • Use the site without being tracked or profiled
  • Contact us with any privacy concerns

6. Contact

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, you can reach out via:

  • Discord: Join our server
  • GitHub: Open an issue

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
