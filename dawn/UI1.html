Frequently Asked Questions

How does Dawn maintain a keyless and free model?
Dawn is funded through community contributions and research sponsorships. We do not place ad-link checkpoints (Linkvertise, LootLabs) or forced payment gates in front of users.
What are sUNC and Myriad Security scores?
sUNC evaluates functional execution behavior across standard environment functions. Myriad is a security benchmark testing host-level isolation to ensure scripts cannot read private local files or execute unauthorized operations outside the sandbox. Dawn scores 100% on both tests.
How does the AI Assistant function?
The integrated AI agent acts as a native copilot inside the UI. It can decompile complex bytecode, analyze runtime errors, and assist in writing or auditing Luau scripts in real time.
What happens when Roblox updates?
Dawn features automated offset scanning that updates signatures in the background, maintaining a 100% uptime average following client patches.
